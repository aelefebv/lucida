# Outputs — Standalone Artifacts

Working documents produced during conversations: migration plans, refactor proposals, decision drafts, post-mortems. Unlike articles, these need not satisfy the article guardrails — they're in-flight or one-shot.

## Article vs output

- **Article** (`systems/`, `decisions/`, `flows/`, `gotchas/`, …) — durable, navigable, guardrail-checked.
- **Output** — in-flight or one-shot: a migration plan executed once, a proposal awaiting review, a decision draft iterated before it becomes a `decisions/` article.

When an output stabilizes into durable knowledge, promote it to an article and remove the output copy.
